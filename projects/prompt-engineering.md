An accessible intro to working with LLMs, written for web developers who want practical workflows, not hype. The course covers prompt design, working with the OpenAI API, and building simple AI-powered features into existing web projects.

The course was distributed across Scrimba, Coursera, and FreeCodeCamp — the same content published across three platforms. The FreeCodeCamp version has 265,000 views.

### What I built

I wrote the curriculum, scripted the lessons, and presented the video content. The course was designed to meet developers where they are — no ML background required, just familiarity with JavaScript and an API or two.

Getting this onto Coursera and FreeCodeCamp meant reaching a much larger audience than a standalone Scrimba course. That distribution was part of the design goal from the start.
